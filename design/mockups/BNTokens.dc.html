Bento
Every panel is its own tile with a 12 px gap. Summary tiles in the four tints sit above the content. Squircle avatars and buttons (radius 12–16). Busier, better at a glance.
Nordic
Rail and folders sit directly on the ground. List, reader and context share one large surface split by hairlines. Everything is a pill or a circle. Calmer, more space for reading.
The style is a setting (Appearance › Style). Colours, type and tints are identical, so dark and light work the same in both.
